# Tooltip
The game's tooltip (StandardTooltip: Storm_UI_Tooltip_TopBorder, flat colours, so drawn in CSS): hrs-tooltip-fill at 242/255 over the page blurred, a 2px hrs-tooltip-edge border at 179/255 with a faint dark edge outside, square. Padding 20/18em, at most 480/18em wide (the game's insets and widest for its 18 high type), 14px text in hrs-tooltip-text with a shadow, the subject in hrs-text. The same in every theme, as the game's. Appears after 300ms on hover and on focus; never holds controls.
**Material:** `mat-tooltip` with `--mat-tooltip-container-color` (the fill), `--mat-tooltip-supporting-text-color: var(--hrs-tooltip-text)`, `--mat-tooltip-container-shape: 0`; its surface (`.mat-mdc-tooltip-surface`) takes the border, edge, blur, padding and width, which Material has no tokens for.
