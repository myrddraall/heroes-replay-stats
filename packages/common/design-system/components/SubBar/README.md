# SubBar
Level 2, directly under the TopBar, built as the game's SubNavContainer and Top_Navigation buttons: a darker blurred film (the tiled `Storm_UI_MainNavigation_SubBackground`, 52 units under the 108 bar) holding the sections of the current area as a tab row.
- Labels: `nav-2` = the game's TaskBarSubNavigation style: Metronic Bold, title case as the game writes them (Versus A.I. | Quick Match | Ranked), gradient text from the theme accent with a dark glow; separated by `Storm_UI_MainNavigation_Separator_Sub` (2×20).
- Selected (`aria-current="page"`): the label goes to the white gradient with the theme glow, and UnderlineImage (`Storm_UI_MainNavigation_Hover_Underline`, 85 units in the 87-unit button) fades in over 0.2 s with its bright line on the band's bottom edge, the tab's width. No pulse and no sparkles on this level (the layout fades its GlowImage to 0).
- Overflow scrolls horizontally with no visible scrollbar; nothing wraps. Hidden when the area has one section. On handset the band shows the breadcrumb instead and the tabs move into the NavTree.
**Consumer provides:** the section list with routes. **Material:** `mat-tab-nav-bar` with the indicator hidden (the underline is this class), or plain anchors with `aria-current`.
