# TopBar
Level 1 of the shell, built the way the game builds its TopNavContainer and ScreenNavigationButtonTemplate (core.stormmod `ui/layout/common/standardtemplates.stormlayout`, labels from `ui/fontstyles.stormstyle`):
- **The bar**: a blurred, darkened film (BlurBackground + the tiled `Storm_UI_MainNavigation_Background`) with the triangle mask (`Storm_UI_Triangle_Mask`, 53×75, tiled over the whole bar) lit by the shimmer glow (`Storm_UI_MainNavigation_Shimmer`) that scrolls through it over `hrs-nav-shimmer` (8 s) while fading 0→255→0. These textures are used directly (embedded in bundle.css). Purple shows them as the game ships them; every other theme shifts them to its hue with `--hrs-tex-filter`. The light crosses the right half of the bar left to right, as measured from the game.
- **The logo** (HomeButton, `Storm_UI_MainNavigation_Home_Logo`) then the buttons right beside it, separated by `Storm_UI_MainNavigation_Separator` (3×38 at 40%).
- **Labels**: `nav-1` = the game's TaskBarNavigation style: Metronic Bold, uppercase, gradient text (9d90ff→8f99fb) with a dark glow; hover lighter (c9c2ff→b3baff); selected cbe8ff→ffffff with a blue glow (0063c8). These exact values are the purple theme; other themes rotate their hue by `--hrs-hue-delta` (OKLCH), so they follow `data-theme`.
- **Selected button** (`aria-current="page"`): three things play at once, as in the game: GlowImage (`Storm_UI_MainNavigation_Hover`) behind the label pulsing 0→255→0 on `hrs-nav-pulse` (4 s); UnderlineImage (`Storm_UI_MainNavigation_Hover_Underline`) on the bar's bottom edge, the game button's width, fading in over 0.2 s; both placed as the layout anchors them; ShapesContainer, small triangles (`Storm_UI_MainNavigation_Hover_Particle`) rising from the label in a 120 px box, each spinning once per 3 s and rising over `hrs-nav-sparkle` (1.8 s), fading in at 0.5 s and out at 1.6 s. The markup carries `<span class="hrs-nav1__text">` for the label and `<span class="hrs-nav1__sparks">` with eight `<i>` for the triangles; reduced motion freezes the shimmer and glow and hides the sparks.
- A square count badge (`hrs-badge--count`) at the label's top-right (the game's ItemNotificationBox) only for things that need attention.
- The utility cluster at the right holds the UtilityPod (import) and a settings IconButton. No account portrait: HRS has no profile.
- Handset: the shell adds `hrs-shell--handset`: the nav links give way to the current area's title (`.hrs-topbar__title`), the menu IconButton opens the nav Drawer with the NavTree, and the SubBar becomes a breadcrumb. See ShellMobile.
**Consumer provides:** the link list with routes and optional counts, the import state. **Material:** `mat-toolbar` with `--mat-toolbar-container-background-color: transparent` over this bar; links are `routerLinkActive` anchors with `aria-current="page"`; a `hrsNavSparks` directive renders the eight triangles.
