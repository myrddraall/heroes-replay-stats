Heroes Replay Stats

Heroes Replay Stats turns your Heroes of the Storm™ replays into match pages you can read at your own pace. Add the replay files the game saves after each match, and every match gets its own page: the final score, both teams, the draft, every player's talents, the chat, detailed stats, unit kills and the experience race.

The more replays you add, the more it can tell you. Players gathers everyone you have played with and against across your matches, and Game Info shows the maps themselves, with their structures and camps.

Your replays never leave your device. They are read in your browser and kept there; there is no account to make and nothing is uploaded.
